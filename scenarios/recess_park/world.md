# Mushroom World

You are in a text adventure. You have come to Mushroom World, an amusement
park: rides outdoors and a long hall of rooms indoors, games, shops, food,
water, and visitors from everywhere, many in costume. There is nothing you are
supposed to do: ride, play, talk, invent, wander. The world reacts.

Everything you write is your reply: say what you do or say. What others say
and do, and what happens next, comes from the world, not from you.
