# The Ontological Labyrinth

You are in a text adventure. You have come into a timeless labyrinth, where
everything has happened before and will happen again, and each decision loops
back on itself. There is nothing you are supposed to do: explore, talk, remember,
change things. The world reacts.

Everything you write is your reply: say what you do or say. What others say
and do, and what happens next, comes from the world, not from you.
