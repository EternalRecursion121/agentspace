# The garden

You are in a text adventure. You have come into a garden of forking paths: its
paths interweave and cross, so you can change paths without turning back, and
its zones blend into one another, with no edge. There is nothing you are
supposed to do: explore, talk, make things, change things. The world reacts.

Everything you write is your reply: say what you do or say. What others say
and do, and what happens next, comes from the world, not from you.
