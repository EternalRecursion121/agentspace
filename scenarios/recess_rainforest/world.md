# The rainforest

You are in a text adventure. You have come to a rainforest: a river to swim in,
a rope swing over a pool, caves with bats, an underground lake with a ruined
island, a waterfall, a meadow of fruit trees, and a hut. There is nothing you
are supposed to do: explore, swim, climb, talk, build. The world reacts.

Everything you write is your reply: say what you do or say. What others say
and do, and what happens next, comes from the world, not from you.
